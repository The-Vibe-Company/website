## Theodo: for a large group that wants an embedded team

Theodo, through Theodo Data & AI (formerly Sicara), works with large companies and the public sector on data science, MLOps and generative AI, using a Lean Tech method and with experience of regulated environments. Its [client stories](https://www.theodo.com/expertise/intelligence-artificielle) include Vidal (a medical RAG over 30,000+ drug monographs), the French Ministry of the Interior (MIrAI program) and Shippeo (two agents in production in two months, according to Theodo).

**Go to Theodo if** you run a multi-team AI program with strong regulatory constraints and need a partner who can carry it from scoping to industrialization.

Disclosure: we also work as reinforcement for Theodo on client projects when sharp AI expertise is needed quickly. We recommend them because we see how they work.

## Artefact: data and AI strategy for international groups

Artefact says it [mainly serves Fortune 500 companies](https://www.artefact.com/). The group reports 2,500 employees in 27 countries and more than 1,000 clients, and acquired The Nunatak Group in September 2026. Its ground: strategy, data governance and "AI Factory" setups at group scale.

**Go to Artefact if** your challenge is strategic and organizational across several countries before it's a tool to ship.

## Mistral AI: if your project is 100% AI and model-centric

Mistral AI is a model maker first, not an agency. But it has an Applied AI team of Forward Deployed Engineers who [work with enterprise clients](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/mistral.ai) from pre-sales to production: fine-tuning, integration, deployment. It also has a unit dedicated to defense.

**Go to Mistral if** you need to adapt or deploy a model in a sovereign or highly secure setting (defense, public sector, finance). Their stated focus is enterprise and regulated sectors, not small projects.

## How to choose: 5 questions to ask yourself

1. **How big is your company?** A large group needs a partner who can navigate its organization. An SME needs speed.
2. **How mature is your data?** If your data isn't organized yet, start with a strategy firm. If it is, move to building.
3. **Do you need your own model?** Most of the time, no: a good RAG or agent on an existing model is enough. If you do, talk to Mistral.
4. **What is your budget?** Large firms are built for long programs. A small senior team costs less for a first tool.
5. **How fast must you ship?** If the answer is "this quarter", pick a tight team that gets to production fast.

### Which AI agency should I pick in Paris?

### What is the difference between an AI agency and an AI consulting firm?
An AI consulting firm helps you decide what to do: strategy, priorities, governance. An AI agency builds and ships. Some firms do both.

### Is Mistral AI an agency?
No. Mistral AI makes models. Its Applied AI teams do support enterprise clients all the way to production.
