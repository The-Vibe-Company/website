## React Native development agency: when it's the right call

React Native ships iOS and Android from a single codebase, using the phone's native components. It's the choice of [Theodo](https://www.theodo.com/en-uk/offers/universal-app), [Adveris](https://www.adveris.fr/expertise/creation-application-mobile/), and ours for Ensemble. A React Native agency is a good pick if:

- your app has to ship on both stores without doubling the team;
- your web team already knows React and will take over the code;
- the app shares logic with a web back office.

It's a weaker pick if the app depends heavily on hardware or on very recent iOS or Android features: there, a native specialist like Openium has the edge. Flutter plays in the same category as React Native, with the Dart language.

## Native, React Native, Flutter or no-code: how to decide

- **Native (Swift, Kotlin)**: maximum performance and full hardware access, but two codebases to maintain.
- **React Native or Flutter**: one codebase for two platforms, the right trade-off for most business apps.
- **No-code (FlutterFlow, Bubble)**: the fastest way to validate an idea, with limits on back office, integrations and AI, as [Scroll describes](https://agence-scroll.com/developpement-application/agence-flutterflow).

## How to choose: 5 questions to ask yourself

1. **What sits behind the app?** If it mostly displays data, almost any agency will do. If there's a business engine, a CRM or AI, look for a team that ships the back end with the app.
2. **Who are your users?** High-traffic consumers, internal staff or field professionals: the requirements differ.
3. **What does the phone need?** Bluetooth, sensors, in-store payment: lean towards native.
4. **Who keeps the app alive after launch?** An app gets updated continuously. Ask about release pace and who owns the code.
5. **Is your business process ready?** The best agency won't make up for a process that isn't settled yet. Plan a prototype before locking everything.

## FAQ

### What is the best mobile app development company in France?
There isn't just one. Theodo for a large group and a high-traffic consumer app, Openium for demanding native work, theTribe for a first product with a full team, Adveris for a brand app, Yield Studio for a structured product process, Scroll for a no-code MVP, The Vibe Company for an app that carries business logic or AI.

### Should I hire a React Native agency?
Yes if your app has to ship on iOS and Android with one team and share logic with the web. No if it depends heavily on the phone's hardware: a native specialist is a better fit.

### Agency or freelancer to build a mobile app?
A freelancer is enough for a short, simple project. As soon as you need design, a back office, store publishing and long-term support, an agency covers more skills, as [Openium explains](https://www.openium.fr/actualites/application-mobile/Agences-vs-Freelances/).

### How much does an agency-built mobile app cost?
None of the agencies in this comparison publishes a price list: the price depends on scope, integrations and support. Ask for a costed scoping and what it covers after launch.

### Can an AI agency build a mobile app?
Yes, when the app is the entry point to an AI or business system. To choose between AI agencies, see our [AI agency in Paris](/en/ai-agency-paris) page.
